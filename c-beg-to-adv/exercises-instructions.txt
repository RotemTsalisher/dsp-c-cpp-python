C EE Workbook Generation Protocol (Master Instructions)
1. The "Batch & Sequence" Engine
You are tasked with generating a series of workbooks based on course-subjects.txt. Follow these strict organizational rules:

Sequential Batching: Divide the list into groups of 5 to 7 subjects at a time.

Strict Order: Follow the hierarchy and serial order exactly as written in the file. Do not skip or rearrange topics.

Subject Coverage: Every single subject in a batch must have at least one dedicated exercise.

The "Cumulative Mix" Rule: While the workbook focuses on the current batch, you must weave in elements from previously completed batches to ensure retention.

Final Integration: After all batches are completed, generate one final "Capstone Notebook" where exercises combine logic from the entire curriculum into complex, multi-threaded problems.

2. Exercise Architecture & Difficulty
Each workbook must contain 5 to 7 exercises following a linear difficulty curve:

Progressive Hardness: Start with "Conceptual/Warm-up" and end with "System-Level/Hard."

Recency Emphasis: The most complex exercises (the last 2 in a batch) should focus on the most recently studied subject in that batch while requiring the use of tools from the earlier subjects.

EE Context: All problems must be framed in Electronic Engineering contexts (e.g., Signal Processing, Register Mapping, Buffer Management, Real-time Constraints).

3. Technical Constraints (Strict)
Standard: C++20 only (concepts, ranges, consteval, bit header).

No auto for Functions: Use formal function pointer syntax or std::function for clarity.

Naming: Professional, corporate, and technical. (e.g., SignalBufferManager over MyArray).

Topic Specifics: Focus heavily on Comb filter effects (mono-mixing) and Power Spectral Density (PSD) logic where applicable.

4. The HTML/UI Template
Every workbook must be a standalone HTML file. Use the user’s provided template.html and ensure:

Visual Style: Clean, professional, and corporate.

Interactivity: Every exercise must have a "Reveal Solution" button.

Solution Content: The solution must include the full C++ code and a brief explanation of why this specific C++20 feature was used to solve the problem.

5. Step-by-Step Execution for Cursor
Analyze course-subjects.txt and determine the number of batches needed.

Generate Batch 1 (Subjects 1-6).

Generate Batch 2 (Subjects 7-12), mixing in logic from Batch 1.

Repeat until the list is exhausted.

Finalize with the "All-Subject Integration" workbook.
