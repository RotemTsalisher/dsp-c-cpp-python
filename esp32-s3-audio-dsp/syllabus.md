# Syllabus — ESP32-S3: Embedded Systems, DSP, and Digital Audio

**Hardware:** ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 (or documented equivalent; note PSRAM variant)

## Global requirements
- 3 progressive exercises + 1 independent assignment per module
- Labs with expected results, test procedures, debugging guidance
- Solutions separate from learner-facing files
- Git-friendly project layout with build/flash/test documented

## Modules

1. **ESP32-S3 architecture and development environment**
2. **Embedded C/C++ and memory architecture**
3. **GPIO, interrupts, and peripheral routing**
4. **Timers and PWM**
5. **FreeRTOS and multitasking**
6. **ADC and analog interfacing**
7. **UART, SPI, and I2C**
8. **DSP foundations on ESP32-S3**
9. **I2S digital audio interface**
10. **DMA-based audio streaming**
11. **FIR, IIR, and FFT on ESP32-S3**
12. **Advanced embedded audio processing**
13. **Final ESP32-S3 audio DSP project**

## Final projects

- Real-time I2S digital audio effects processor
- Multi-band parametric equalizer
- FFT spectrum analyzer with I2S input
- I2S acquisition + Wi-Fi streaming
